A stereo sound system built around distributed-mode vibration panels. The work
began with an accidental tabletop experiment and is now a sequence of material,
mounting, and response-measurement experiments.

## Current state

The MDF-and-plywood panel on a compliant aluminium frame gives unexpectedly
strong bass and a promising midrange response. High frequencies remain the
main unresolved limitation.

## Next question

Can an aluminium plate between the exciter and MDF improve high-frequency
behaviour without losing the properties that made the current construction work?
